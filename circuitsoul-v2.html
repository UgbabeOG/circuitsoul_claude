CircuitSoul Shield

AI-Driven Cybersecurity & Compliance for African Startups

The Problem

African startups face sophisticated cyber threats without the budget for dedicated security teams or enterprise-grade tools. Compliance requirements — NDPR, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS — create additional overhead that slows growth and increases investor friction.

How Shield Works

Shield deploys lightweight monitoring agents across your infrastructure and continuously analyzes network traffic, access patterns, and system configurations against a real-time threat intelligence database. Its AI layer correlates signals across your entire environment to detect threats before they escalate, and generates compliance documentation automatically.

Core Capabilities

  • Continuous vulnerability scanning across cloud and on-premise infrastructure
  • AI-driven anomaly detection with contextual risk scoring per asset
  • Real-time cybersecurity posture dashboard with investor-ready reporting
  • Automated compliance mapping: NDPR, ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI-DSS
  • Security incident alerting with structured remediation guidance
  • Third-party vendor risk assessment and supply chain visibility
  • API security monitoring and credential exposure detection

CircuitSoul CloudOps

Cloud Infrastructure Management & DevSecOps Platform

The Problem

Scaling African startups face mounting cloud costs, fragmented tooling, and insecure deployment pipelines. Engineering teams spend disproportionate time on infrastructure management rather than product development, and security is often retrofitted after the fact.

How CloudOps Works

CloudOps integrates directly with your cloud provider APIs and CI/CD pipelines to create a unified operational layer. It monitors infrastructure health, tracks cost allocation by service and team, enforces security policies in deployment workflows, and surfaces actionable optimization recommendations through a centralized console.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-cloud infrastructure monitoring across AWS, GCP, and Azure
  • Intelligent cost allocation, forecasting, and anomaly detection
  • DevOps pipeline visualization and deployment velocity analytics
  • DevSecOps policy enforcement: security gates in CI/CD workflows
  • Infrastructure-as-Code drift detection and configuration auditing
  • Container and Kubernetes cluster management and optimization
  • Performance analytics with SLA tracking and capacity planning

CircuitSoul Flow

Workflow Automation & Internal Tools Platform

The Problem

African SMEs and startups lose enormous productivity to manual, repetitive processes. Building custom internal tools requires engineering resources most companies cannot justify. The result is operational bottlenecks that throttle growth at every stage.

How Flow Works

Flow provides a visual workflow engine connected to your business systems through a library of pre-built integrations. Teams automate multi-step processes, build custom operational dashboards, and connect applications without engineering overhead on every operational change. A developer SDK enables custom internal tools built on the platform's infrastructure layer.

Core Capabilities

  • Visual workflow builder for no-code process automation
  • Pre-built connectors for 100+ business applications and APIs
  • Custom internal dashboards and operational reporting interfaces
  • Multi-system data synchronization with transformation rules
  • Process analytics: bottleneck identification and efficiency metrics
  • Approval workflows with multi-stakeholder routing and audit trails
  • Developer SDK for extending Flow with custom business logic

Our Mission

CircuitSoul is building the digital infrastructure that powers Africa's next generation of startups and technology-driven businesses. We believe the critical gap in Africa's technology ecosystem is not talent or capital — it is infrastructure.

African startups deserve access to the same caliber of security, cloud operations, and automation tooling available to their counterparts in New York, London, or Singapore — engineered specifically for the African operating environment: bandwidth constraints, regulatory context, and local business logic.

CircuitSoul is a Nigerian technology company committed to building the software backbone that eliminates infrastructure as a constraint on African business growth.
